# Maintenance Checks

Before a demo or release:

- Verify payment events are processed idempotently.
- Confirm failed or refunded events do not increase request limits.
- Review demo conversations and screenshots for personal family details.
- Ensure support logs contain statuses and references, not full message text.
- Confirm expired access is enforced consistently across text and voice requests.
- Reset demo accounts to synthetic data and documented limits before recording screenshots.
- Test that an account deletion request removes or anonymizes linked conversation and billing references as documented.
- Verify parental guidance examples are clearly presented as informational, not professional advice.
- Confirm restored accounts recalculate remaining request limits from authoritative billing records.
- Confirm demo billing records use synthetic references and cannot trigger real payment actions.
